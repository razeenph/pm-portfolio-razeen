# Gig Economy Credit Scoring – Fintech Simulation

## Overview
GigScore Arena is a fintech simulation platform designed to evaluate creditworthiness of gig economy workers using alternative data.

Traditional credit scoring models struggle with gig workers due to volatile income and lack of formal documentation.

This project designs a hybrid credit model using platform behavioral data and income stability metrics.

---

## Problem

Gig economy workers often face financial exclusion because:

- Income is highly volatile
- Lack of formal employment records
- Limited credit history

Traditional credit scoring systems cannot properly assess their risk.

---

## Solution

Develop an alternative credit scoring framework using gig platform data.

Key variables:

- Earnings stability
- Customer ratings
- Completion rate
- Platform tenure
- Activity patterns

---

## Credit Model Framework

Core metrics used:

• Income Stability Score  
• Behavioral Reliability Indicators  
• Risk-weighted portfolio scoring  
• Compliance with RBI digital lending norms  

---

## Platform Data Signals

Examples of data used for credit scoring:

- Weekly earnings consistency
- Acceptance rate
- Cancellation rate
- Platform tenure
- Customer rating
- Active days per month

These indicators help measure repayment capacity and behavioral reliability.

---

## Product Vision

GigScore Arena acts as a **Fintech Risk Laboratory** where students and analysts can simulate lending decisions using alternative data models.
